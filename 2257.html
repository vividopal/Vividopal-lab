18 U.S.C. § 2257 Compliance Statement

Effective Date: March 22, 2026

1. Statutory Compliance

Vivid Opal Lab LLC ("the Company") is in full compliance with the record-keeping requirements of 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and its implementing regulations at 28 C.F.R. Part 75.

All visual depictions of sexually explicit conduct displayed on platforms managed by Vivid Opal Lab LLC feature only persons who were at least eighteen (18) years of age at the time of the creation of such depictions.

3. Exemption Statement

Vivid Opal Lab LLC operates as a content management agency. The Company does not directly produce primary visual depictions of sexually explicit conduct. In accordance with 28 C.F.R. § 75.8, the Company maintains secondary producer records for all managed content creators.

4. Record Retention

All records required under 18 U.S.C. § 2257 are retained for a minimum period of seven (7) years from the date of production of the visual depiction, or five (5) years from the last date the content was sold, distributed, or otherwise disseminated — whichever period is longer.

Records are maintained in an encrypted, access-controlled environment and are available for inspection by authorised officials upon lawful request within seventy-two (72) hours.
