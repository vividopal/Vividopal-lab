01

Services

Vivid Opal Lab LLC ("VividOpal" or "the Company") provides digital brand management services to independent content creators. Services include account management, content post-production, audience engagement, promotional strategy, and financial settlement.

The Company operates strictly as an independent contractor and does not establish any employment or fiduciary relationship with creators. All creators are engaged under separate Independent Contractor Agreements.

02

Commission & Payment

VividOpal operates on a performance-based commission model. No upfront fees, registration fees, or advance payments are charged under any circumstances.

Commission rates are structured on a tiered basis that improves in favour of the creator over time, as outlined in each individual contractor agreement. All payments are processed via secure financial channels and settled monthly to the creator's designated bank account.
